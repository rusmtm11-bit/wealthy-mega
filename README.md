# Wealthy Mega Trade Limited

Corporate multi-page website for **Wealthy Mega Trade Limited**, a Hong Kong-based general trading company.

## Pages

- Home — company introduction and global trading partner positioning
- About — who we are, commitment, and reasons to choose WMT
- Products & Industries — 14 supplied product and industry categories
- Services — sourcing & procurement, logistics & distribution, regulatory compliance & quality control
- Contact — Hong Kong office details and `info@wealthymega.com`

## GitHub Pages

Publish the contents of this repository from the `main` branch and the repository root.

Required root structure:

```text
index.html
about.html
products.html
services.html
contact.html
assets/
css/
js/
.nojekyll
```

`markets.html` is retained only as a redirect to `products.html` for older bookmarks.
